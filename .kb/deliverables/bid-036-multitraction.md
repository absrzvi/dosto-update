# Bid TD v0.4 (multitraction) — ND-BID-OBB-036

**Resource:** `/ND-BID-OBB-036_Technical description_Dosto_Neu-v0.4_multitraction.docx`
(do not deep-parse the binary here).

The **PM-03-era concept-design** Technical Description from the bid phase. Its chapter 6 defines the
**"required information" checklist** that the design-freeze (PM-04) is assessed against — physical
topology, VLAN list, IP scheme (Nomad + Stadler), network services, firewall policy, and allowed
wayside connections. The delivery SDD (`ND-DEL-OBB-035-SDD-002-01 v2.2`) is the post-sale successor
that matures and freezes this content.

The **multitraction** variant is the relevant one for coupled-train (multitraction) operation — the
scope behind the RSTP coupler-cost work and the coupling tests.
