CCU Stability — Single Traction
Finding 1 — Modem / SIM instability
Root cause: CPDL hardware fault on modem carrier blades — first two SIM slots fail to come up, reducing redundant dual-SIM operation to a single SIM.
Fix: blade replacement. 4 trains completed to date (2 by SPL, 2 by Nomad); remaining fleet count and completion target [ Abbas: to confirm ].
Finding 2 — Power resets (ignition-sense signature)
Repeatable signature captured via hardened CCU logging on 4736-110: ignition-sense voltage drops to 0V while main input voltage stays stable; ignition reads 0 for ~90s, then the CCU hard-cuts. Three occurrences captured, 16–18 June 2026.
Status: root cause narrowed to the ignition-sense input, not the power supply. Investigation ongoing — not yet resolved.
Other items raised in ÖBB's 10 March test protocol (client isolation on staff WLAN, switch-visibility during the incident) remain open pending design freeze and functionality testing completion — see slide 10.

Multi-Traction Roadmap
✓ Found and fixed, fast — Nomad-owned
12 June coupling test surfaced a perpetual ~2s RSTP topology-change storm on coupled 6+6 consists. Root cause: asymmetric coupler port-costs in Nomad's own v8 template. Fixed in v9 (symmetric cost 20000, max-age raised to 38, native-VLAN containment) — validated stable across all 2-train orientations by the 30 June re-test.
✓ CCTV / ZFR degradation — resolved, same fix
The 30 June test isolated the variable: the v9 cost fix alone — no firewall change, no VLAN-5 change — stopped the churn and the CCTV feed recovered immediately. This revises the earlier hypothesis: the degradation was driven by the RSTP storm flooding camera traffic, not by any Stadler firewall interaction. No further Stadler dependency on this item.
Roadmap input: triple traction (3×6, 54 switches) exceeds RSTP's node/diameter limits — requires an L3 / routed segmentation redesign if triple traction is ever required. Flagged now, not a current defect.
This work was delivered as goodwill, outside contracted scope, and is not on Nomad's committed phase plan — see Scope & VO Status, slide 9.
