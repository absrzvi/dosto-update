## Method (how channel was read)

- SNMP (v3, user `admin`/SHA1/AES128/`NomadStayOut!`) **does not expose radio channel** on IbexOS
  (Westermo Ibex 6.11.2-0) — only an RSSI/WLINK status block under `.1.3.6.1.4.1.16177`.
- LuCI data-view (`/cgi-bin/luci/admin/status/general/data?view=wireless`) answers on only
  ~5 of 16 APs (firmware-build dependent) — unreliable.
- **Authoritative path used: `iw dev` over SSH** (`nomad`/`NomadComeIn`, legacy KEX) on each AP.
- AP→physical-slot binding: VDS switch MAC table (`show mac`, grep AP MAC on `e0-4`/`e1-2`),
  switch→coach via hostname (`show running-config` → `system hostname nv4-<pos>-v8-021`).
- Config variant (m / non-m) read from DHCP lease hostname suffix (`APNm-v1` vs `APN-v1`).

## Customer correlation

Customer MAC suffix = the AP's wlan1 BSSID (device MAC + 3). All 8 customer-reported channels
match the live measurement exactly — the customer's table is accurate, not a misreading.

## Root cause

This is a **band-plan that does not alternate per coach** — NOT cabling, NOT a CCU/OBN runtime fault.

- The mirror was introduced by commit `868f189` (Davud Z, 2026-04-07) "Mirrored x1-x3, x2-x4 AP
  configuration after coach 2" to stagger 5 GHz between adjacent coaches.
- It split AP rules into `coach_number: [1,2]` (non-m) and `[3,4]` (m) — a **front-half / back-half**
  grouping, present identically in the upstream template
  (`nomad-obn-template-nv4/src/etc/obn/rules.yaml`) and on the live CCU.
- For per-coach co-channel staggering to actually work, the variant must **alternate every coach**
  (non-m / m / non-m / m). The deployed `[1,2]/[3,4]` grouping puts the SAME plan on adjacent
  coaches A&G and on E&B → staggering fails at the A–G and E–B boundaries.
- `nv4-topology.md` line 82 states the boundary as "coaches 1,4 non-m; 2,3 m" (alternating).
  **rules.yaml disagrees with that and is the defect.**

OBN applied rules.yaml faithfully; the defect is in the rules.yaml coach grouping in the
canonical nv4 template → **affects the entire 4734 fleet** (matches prior reports on Fzg 19, 20, 21).

## Supersedes

The 2026-06-03 Fzg 19 conclusion ("rules.yaml AP*m-v1 wirelessFreq mis-assigned to 2.4 GHz")
is **incorrect**. rules.yaml is internally self-consistent — each `APNm-v1` band is a deliberate
mirror of `APN-v1`. The real defect is the **`[1,2]/[3,4]` coach grouping** (half-split instead
of per-coach alternation).
